## TECHNOLOGY - WHAT THEY SAY

### Core Concept
**"Wish-to-game" technology** - "allows anyone to bring interactive ideas to life—instantly—with nothing more than natural language"

### How It Works (According to Them)

**Original Launch (April 2025):**
> "We built a suite of specialized AI models that can create everything from lush landscapes to fully animated characters—living, breathing things, ready to play with—from nothing more than descriptions."

> "A proprietary model called AstroBrain that orchestrates the other models, seamlessly handling the countless details required to fulfill your creative vision without human intervention."

**Astrocade 2.0 (July 2025):**
> "Astrocade isn't just one AI anymore, but a whole team of specialized AI agents. Each has a unique role, handling visuals, crafting intuitive UI, fine-tuning gameplay and feel, all while collaborating seamlessly."

### Asset Generation Approach

**From their blog posts:**
> "Astrocade translates natural language descriptions into interactive worlds, automating the generation of art, animation and visual effects, music and sound, [and] even custom gameplay mechanics."

> "We fine tune our AI models on original work created by our growing team of compensated artists and animators."

> "Our platform doesn't allow users to directly download model-generated assets, ensuring they can't be used to replace artists working on games outside of our platform."

### Animation System

**From their Animation Cookbook (October 2025):**
> "Astrocade uses sprite sheets to represent character or object animations. Unlike 3D skeletal animation or Spine-based 2D animation, a sprite sheet is composed of a series of animation frames, each representing one frame of a sequence."

**Process:**
1. User has a static image of a character/object
2. User asks Astrocade to create an animation (e.g., "walking", "attacking")
3. User specifies when to play that animation
4. Astrocade generates sprite sheet frames
5. User can iterate with follow-up prompts to adjust size, speed, perspective

**Known Limitations (They Admit):**
- "Astrocade doesn't handle very fast or short animations well yet, such as a quick sword swing"
- Complex multi-part animations may require additional refinement

### Generation Time
> "Games can be created in mere minutes"
- User reports suggest 2-5 minutes per game
- vs GameTOK's 10-30 seconds
